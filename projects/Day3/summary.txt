LLM COMES IN 3 FLAVORS

1.Base model 

  - Predicts the next sequence 
  - Example: In mobile - when you type "hello", get/ predict the next sequence after "hello hai" 
  - Before Chatgpt INTRODUCED IN 2022, the previous model GPT 3 is the "base model". It also used the q:question , a:answers train then - we can give ques & answer in this way. 
  - Used when training a model to do something different , to give it a new skill

2. Chat / Instruct variant  ( also a hybrid model - decide how much thinking to do a task )
   
   - OpenAi then changed it to one message then its response - trained to work in prompt style.
   - Then, One info called chat - system prompt,user prompt. This approach used Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). 
   - This formed the normal GPT to Chat GPT
   - fast bcoz of no reasoning, cheaper
   - Used for Content generation - write email

3.Reasoning / Thinking model (also a hybrid model)

   - Trick - Prompt engineerning Ex:ask model to do something then adding at last: check step by step (prompt)..it works 
   - Train the model to first output the thinking step and  give the answer 
   - Example : OpenAi OSS model thinking things before giving its reply  
   - Reasoning Budget/Effort : Amount of reasoning a model does
   - Budget Forcing : when you make a reasoning model, think longer,you need to make it more reasoning.. ie, reason a bit deeper 
( add "weight" to the thinking tract - then the model will do , wait I should rethink this, am I sure ? let me reconsider) 
   - Reasoning model with high Reasoning Budget PERFORM BETTER in all diff benchmarks..
   - Used for proble solving, better at puzzles | Highly intelligent
